# Daily Word — Site Objective

## What Is Daily Word?

Daily Word is a Catholic devotional website that presents the Roman Catholic Mass readings for each day of the liturgical calendar alongside AI-generated reflections designed to make Scripture more accessible, meaningful, and applicable to everyday life.

## The Problem It Solves

The Bible — especially in its liturgical translations — is written in language shaped by ancient cultures, poetry, and theological traditions that can feel distant or difficult to understand for many modern readers. A passage from Lamentations or a parable from the Gospels may carry profound meaning, but without context and explanation, that meaning can be lost.

The Catholic Church assigns specific readings for every single day of the year through the Roman Lectionary. Most Catholics hear these readings at Mass but may not fully grasp what God is communicating through them, or how to carry that message into the rest of their day.

## The Solution

Daily Word bridges that gap by:

1. **Automatically pulling the day's readings** from the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB) each morning at midnight CST — including the First Reading, Responsorial Psalm, and Gospel.

2. **Generating a thoughtful AI reflection** for each reading that includes:
   - A plain-English summary of what the passage is actually saying
   - The key messages and truths God is communicating
   - Historical and cultural context that deepens understanding
   - Practical ways to apply the reading's message to daily modern life

3. **Presenting a unified daily message** that ties all of the day's readings together into a single spiritual theme — helping readers begin their day with focus and intention.

4. **Archiving every day's readings and reflections** so users can revisit past dates, follow along with the liturgical year, and build a personal record of daily Scripture engagement.

## Who It's For

Daily Word is for any Catholic — or anyone curious about Catholic Scripture — who wants to:

- Understand the daily Mass readings more deeply
- Prepare for or reflect after attending Mass
- Engage with Scripture daily without needing a theology degree
- Build a consistent habit of faith-centered reading and reflection

## Core Values

- **Faithful** — Reflections are grounded in Catholic teaching and tradition.
- **Accessible** — No jargon. No assumptions. Scripture explained as if speaking to a friend.
- **Daily** — Automatically updated every day, following the Church's liturgical calendar.
- **Contemplative** — Designed to slow you down, not scroll past. The site's aesthetic reflects the reverence the Word deserves.

## What It Is Not

Daily Word is not a replacement for Mass, the Catechism, or official Church teaching. The AI reflections are aids to personal contemplation — starting points for prayer and meditation, not authoritative theological commentary.
